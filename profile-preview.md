      <span class="section-label">RESEARCH DIRECTION</span>
      <h2>A question worth pursuing.</h2>
    </div>
  </div>

  <!-- Central research question -->
  <div class="research-question">
    <span class="question-label">GUIDING QUESTION</span>
    <p>
      How can we develop
      <em>generalizable, multimodal AI foundation models</em>
      that learn across molecular, cellular, and biomedical
      imaging data to understand biological systems and
      accelerate scientific discovery?
    </p>
  </div>

  <!-- Connection to research interests -->
  <p class="research-intro">
    To pursue this question, I aim to develop and study
    machine learning models through three interconnected
    research interests:
  </p>

  <!-- Three research cards -->
  <div class="research-grid">

    <article class="research-card">
      <h3>Biological Representation Learning &amp; Foundation Models</h3>
      <p class="research-card-question">
        What transferable patterns can models learn across
        DNA, RNA, proteins, molecular graphs, and cells?
      </p>
      <p>
        Interested in self-supervised learning, graph
        representations, and foundation models that capture
        biological structure and relationships across contexts.
      </p>
      <div class="research-tags">
        <span>Self-supervised learning</span>
        <span>GNNs</span>
        <span>Foundation models</span>
      </div>
    </article>

    <article class="research-card">
      <h3>Multimodal Learning &amp; Biomedical Computer Vision</h3>
      <p class="research-card-question">
        How can visual, molecular, and clinical evidence
        inform one another?
      </p>
      <p>
        Interested in medical images (MRI, CT, X-ray,
        pathology), cellular microscopy, and methods for
        combining imaging with relevant biological
        or clinical data.
      </p>
      <div class="research-tags">
        <span>Computer vision</span>
        <span>Medical imaging</span>
        <span>Multimodal learning</span>
      </div>
    </article>

    <article class="research-card">
      <h3>Reliable AI for Scientific Discovery</h3>
      <p class="research-card-question">
        When can model predictions generalize and support
        scientifically testable hypotheses?
      </p>
      <p>
        Interested in robustness, distribution shift,
        uncertainty estimation, and rigorous evaluation
        as foundations for dependable scientific
        machine learning.
      </p>
      <div class="research-tags">
        <span>Generalization</span>
        <span>Uncertainty</span>
        <span>Reproducibility</span>
      </div>
    </article>

  </div>

</section>
