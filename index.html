01RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

A question worth pursuing.

GUIDING QUESTION
How can we develop generalizable, multimodal AI foundation models that learn across molecular, cellular, and biomedical imaging data to understand biological systems and accelerate scientific discovery?

To pursue this question, I aim to develop and study machine learning models through three interconnected research interests: learning transferable biological representations, integrating visual and molecular evidence, and building reliable approaches to scientific prediction.

01

Biological Representation Learning & Foundation Models

What transferable patterns can models learn across DNA, RNA, proteins, molecular graphs, and cells?

Interested in self-supervised learning, graph representations, and foundation models that capture biological structure and relationships across contexts.

Self-supervised learningGNNsFoundation models
02

Multimodal Learning & Biomedical Computer Vision

How can visual, molecular, and clinical evidence inform one another?

Interested in medical images (MRI, CT, X-ray, pathology), cellular microscopy, and methods for combining imaging with relevant biological or clinical data.

Computer visionMedical imagingMultimodal learning
03

Reliable AI for Scientific Discovery

When can model predictions generalize and support scientifically testable hypotheses?

Interested in robustness, distribution shift, uncertainty estimation, and rigorous evaluation as foundations for dependable scientific machine learning.

GeneralizationUncertaintyReproducibility

Exploratory interest: Quantum machine learning methods where they may offer meaningful advantages for biomedical modeling.
